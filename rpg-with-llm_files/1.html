This thesis project (completed in 6 months) explores how Large Language Models (LLMs) can be used to generate dynamic, immersive and personalized role-playing game (RPG) adventures in real time. I developed an application in Unity where players can immerse themselves in an entirely AI-generated experience based on their choices, both in terms of narrative and visuals.

I want to try!

Watch the teaser!

(or the demo at the bottom of the page)

🧠Choose your favorite AI !

Each AI acts as a game master, capable of adapting to the player's choices, generating rich dialogues, complex situations and maintaining the coherence and commitment of the story.


🎨 Players are free to choose or create the universe they wish to explore

A medieval world inspired by Dungeons and Dragons, a space epic like Star Wars, or even a totally absurd adventure with SpongeBob SquarePants - anything is possible. The AI adjusts the story, characters and events to match the chosen style and tone..


🖼️ Generates not only the story, but also the images.

To enhance the experience, AI-generated images illustrate locations, characters and key moments in the story, making the journey even more immersive and visually appealing.


📚 The main aim of this thesis :  

to determine whether LLMs are intelligent enough to take on the role of game master. It aims to compare the narrative capabilities of different models, analyze their coherence and creativity, and evaluate players' reaction to the use of AI in video games.


🧠An intelligent enemy system

The llm will make the enemy reason. By playing his role to heart, taking into account the type of enemy he is, his strategy will increase or decrease. For the player, the enemy's behavior seems coherent and realistic.
